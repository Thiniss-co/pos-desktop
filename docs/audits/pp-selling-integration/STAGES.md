### Finding: preserved catalog guard, not a stock rule

A sale rung **after** a server-side product edit (such as a `track_stock` toggle through `UpdateProductAction`), under the previously issued catalog, commits locally. Its upload is then refused with the existing `DESKTOP_CATALOG_REVISION_INVALID`, because the edit archives the sellable revision at that instant. This happens in allocation mode too. It is recorded as an existing limitation and was not changed: catalog, price and tax validation must stay. The D1 symptom from G0 (checkout blocked by a foreground top-up refusal) is gone; that sale now completes.

  - A sample is refused when the round trip exceeds 5 s, the time does not parse, or it arrives out of order.
  - The lower bound is `S + age − age·100 ppm`. It becomes unusable after 6 h, on `powerMonitor` suspend or resume, and on restart. The wall clock is never read.
- **v3 send gate** (worker).
  - A `client_contract_version: 3` item is sent only when a usable bound exists **and** `sold_at ≤ L + 55 s`.
  - Otherwise it is skipped **inside the claim transaction**. Row, bytes, hash, key, attempt count and `next_attempt_at` are all untouched.
  - The item wakes after `max(1 s, sold_at − 55 s − L)`. With no sample, one probe is requested (`connectivity.ensureFresh(0)`), with a 30 s → 5 min fallback wake.
  - v2 items are never gated.
- **Allocation-chain dependencies** (`src/main/repositories/uploadDependency.repository.ts`).
  - A candidate's first consumption `s` on each `(allocation, rights_generation)` chain needs **server evidence** that the external predecessor `s − 1` was applied. Accepted evidence:
    - that consumption row is `acknowledged`;
    - the predecessor's upload is `synced`;
    - a coverage boundary reaches `s − 1` **with a matching chain hash**;
    - a disposition chain-break hold exists (PS6b).
  - Several consumptions of one chain in one invoice need only the external predecessor, and must be contiguous; a gap is reported as `non-contiguous`.
  - No local predecessor means nothing to wait for. A different rights generation is a separate chain.
  - Nothing is renumbered, re-keyed, rewritten or requeued.
- **Claim order** is `queue_sequence` (commit order), no longer the wall-clock `created_at`. `claimNextInvoiceUpload(owner, now, accept)` walks candidates in that order and leases the first accepted one, so unrelated invoices are never blocked.
- **Wake recompute** after every drain:
  - The single timer is armed to the earliest **future** deadline: persisted `retryable_error.next_attempt_at` (new `nextRetryDeadline`, strictly after now), the in-memory time deferral, or the sample retry.
  - Arms are capped at 5 min and recomputed when they fire.
  - A paused worker arms **nothing**; access, session and connectivity events resume it.
  - Held dependents contribute no deadline. Their release events call `requestRun()`: a predecessor syncing within the same drain, a bootstrap (access publish), a top-up (`onGrantsChanged`, new), or a new time sample (`estimator.onSample`, new).
- **`Retry-After`**: a retryable upload failure waits `max(backoff, Retry-After)`.
- **Support item `upload-held-by-predecessor`** in Sync → *Needs support*. It carries the held invoice's `IN-…` reference and the predecessor's (`relatedReference`, shown as "Waiting behind"). EN/AR copy was added. It is listed only for a terminal predecessor (rejected/conflict) or a sequence gap; a retrying predecessor is not a support item.
- The worker's decisions (`upload-held …`, `upload-time-deferred …`) are logged under `POS_API_TRACE`, like the other main services.

- **`RecordOpeningStockAction`.** Reuses `CreateInventoryAdjustmentAction` (`opening_balance`, one `in` line, unit cost null).
  - It takes companies X, then rechecks the warehouse with `WarehouseAvailabilityGuard` (S lock).
  - It reads the product without a lock, then checks eligibility.
  - Retries happen only when it is the outermost transaction.
  - A duplicate key is translated to a 409 only for `stock_items_company_id_product_id_warehouse_id_unique`, and only after rollback.
- **`CreateProductWithOpeningStockAction`.** Takes companies X before the insert; product creation and opening stock are all-or-nothing.
- **`OpeningStockEligibility`.** No stock row (even all-zero), and no movement, count line, adjustment or receiving line, allocation, sale or refund line, or position event at the natural position. Otherwise `409 OPENING_STOCK_POSITION_USED` (new code) tells the owner to use a receiving or an adjustment.
- **`stock_item_position_events`** (new table):
  - FKs `RESTRICT`; `stock_item_id` has no FK. `InsertOnly`.
  - One writer, `StockItemPositionHistory`, which asserts a caller-owned transaction.
  - Written by `CreateStockItemAction` (now inside a transaction), `InventoryOperationService::createStockItem` (now asserts a caller transaction) and `UpdateStockItemAction` (on product or warehouse change).
  - An architecture test pins the writers.
  - **Not backfilled.** A position used before rollout by a direct create later moved away leaves no recorded history. This is a disclosed blind spot.
- **Request identity.** New `owner_operation_requests` table and `OwnerOperationRequestService`, following the `stock_allocation_requests` pattern (serializing company lock, locked lookup, replay or `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`):
  - The `Idempotency-Key` is bound to tenant, actor, operation and the canonical payload as sent.
  - **Every key has one durable outcome:** `recorded` (2xx, stored with the write) or `refused` (422, or a business 409, stored after the rollback under the company lock). A late copy of the original replays the stored outcome and can never commit.
  - A keyed request that fails validation replays an existing outcome, so the original's own commit cannot turn into a misleading 422.
  - Header `Idempotency-Outcome`.
  - `GET operation-requests/{key}` returns `recorded`, `refused` or `not_recorded`; `not_recorded` is never treated as evidence.
- **Owner API:**
  - `POST opening-stock`: `company.feature:inventory,inventory.adjust` plus `InventoryOperationPolicy`, the same gate as company `inventory-adjustments`.
  - Optional `opening_stock_*` fields on `POST products`, all-or-none; a warehouse must be active and company-owned; tracked products only. The request adds `FeaturePermissionGate::ensure` when the fields are present.
  - Owner allowlists for `opening-stock` and `operation-requests`; the route and permission-matrix scans are clean.
- **`inventory:diagnose-allocation {company} {--product=*} {--devices=25} {--limit=25} {--requests=200} {--json}`** (read-only):
  - **Verdicts:** `NO_STOCK_ITEM`, `ZERO_RECORDED_ALLOCATABLE`, `RECORDED_ALLOCATABLE_STOCK`. The output states this is not proof of physical availability and does not guarantee a grant.
  - **Context only:** PP/allocation policy and open quarantines. Zero allocatable is never presented as a PP blocker.
  - **Decisions:** matched to the request's own warehouse and product, read from the stored response.
  - **Bounds:** every input is capped and out-of-range values are refused. There is no Cartesian query.
- **Docs:** `company-owner-endpoint-inventory.md` and `company-owner-modules.md` (row 7d, gates).

**Owner SPA (pos-backend/resources/company-owner)**
- **Create page** (`ProductFormPage`, the only create entry point): an optional "Opening stock" section.
  - Gated by the one shared predicate `openingStockBlock()`: feature `inventory`, plus `inventory.adjust`, plus `inventory.view` or `inventory.manage`. Missing feature, missing adjust and adjust-only each get their own note.
  - Active-only warehouse search; unticking tracking clears the fields.
  - Zero-cost and no-revaluation copy.
- **Edit page:** a "Stock" tab with "Stock by warehouse — current recorded state" (never attributed to a request) and an "Add opening stock" dialog.
- **Keyed submissions** (`useOperationAttempt`, `utils/operationAttempt.ts`):
  - **Definitive evidence:** a 2xx, a server-marked or stored `refused`, or any 4xx to the first send of a fresh key.
  - **Unresolved:** everything else, including a lost answer, a 5xx, `not_recorded`, or a 403/419 on a resend. The form stays locked (persisted per tab across reloads) and offers only "Check outcome" or "Send the same request again". There is no automatic retry and no replacement submission.
- **Copy:** EN/AR in `i18n/parts/openingStock.ts`, with the parity test.

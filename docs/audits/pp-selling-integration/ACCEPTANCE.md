**Kinds**

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **automated** | vitest, Pest, or the Electron real-SQLite suites |
| **live** | the real Electron app driven by Playwright against a guarded disposable Laravel, or the cp3g5 / PS7 live gates |

Journeys live under `tests/playwright/journeys/`, with evidence under `playwright/<journey>/`. The final re-run of every journey on the final source is listed in [STAGES.md](STAGES.md), Stage 9.
